About

Nicole Tecchio is a young illustrator based between Mantua and Verona.
Her studies base on art history and editorial illustration, in particular on kids illustration. She graduated in Art History (DAMS) at Alma Mater Studiorum and in Editorial Illustration at ABABO in Bologna, both cum laude. Currently, she is attending an Editorial master at University of Verona.

She is a person with a lot of interests: music, jazz, collecting sticks and stones, puzzles. Her great ability is to blend all these interests into her illustrations, using her skills to give a new point of view on the world, always with a touch of humor and irony.
She works as librarian in Mantua and Verona areas, especially with children and reading promotion. She visits libraries and schoolsto do laboratories about her books, draws, printing techniques and music.

She published two illustrated books with Quinto Quarto Edizioni.
